# Tide

> Tide is a UK business finance and admin platform ("built by business owners for business owners") serving over 2 million small businesses, sole traders, freelancers, and limited companies. It combines a business current account (e-money account powered by ClearBank, FSCS considerations apply), expense cards, invoicing and payment tools (Tap to Pay, card readers, payment links), MTD-ready accounting and admin, business credit and finance, company formation, and business insurance. Tide is authorised and regulated in relation to its regulated activities by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Tide does not currently publish a public developer API or developer portal.
